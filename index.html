Mythri is a voice-based AI health companion for South Asian women navigating perimenopause. She listens and speaks in Telugu, Urdu, and English, because health information should exist in the languages women actually speak.

Talk to Mythri How it scales
The Problem

Perimenopause affects every woman. For millions of South Asian women, it happens in silence.

  • Doctors dismiss symptoms as "stress" or "just getting older."
  • Families don't talk about it.
  • Health information in Telugu or Urdu is nearly nonexistent.
  • Women endure hot flashes, sleepless nights, mood changes, and joint pain alone, without language for what they are experiencing.

Mythri was built to close that gap.

Meet Mythri

Mythri (మైత్రి) means friendship.

A woman taps the flower, speaks in her language, and Mythri responds: warm, spoken, culturally informed, and free of judgment. Like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know a lot about women's health.

Every response follows the Actionable Empathy framework:

What It Does

Speak, don't type. No app install. No keyboard needed.

🎙️Voice in, voice out

Designed for women who prefer speaking over typing, including those who cannot read or write comfortably in any language.

🌐Three languages, done right

Telugu, Urdu, and English with culturally appropriate honorifics (Meeru / Aap / You).

🧠Symptom-aware brain

Detects which symptom cluster a woman is describing and routes to the correct guidance. Hot flash tips never appear for joint pain.

🏮Culturally grounded

NFHS-5 data, Indian menopause age norms, and South Asian nutrition: methi, flaxseed, turmeric, ghee, sesame, ragi.

📱Zero-friction access

Opens in any mobile browser, straight from a WhatsApp link. No download, no signup wall.

🩺Knows its limits

Mythri is not a doctor. She directs women to medical care when symptoms are severe, new, or worsening.

The Engine

Zero-hallucination health guidance by design.

A server-side detection engine identifies the symptom cluster before the AI is ever called. Each cluster maps to specific, clinically sourced, hard-coded guidance, eliminating generic responses and hallucination risk. Detection works across English, Telugu script, and Urdu script.

Growth

How Mythri scales.

Mythri is a digital-native product delivered entirely through the browser: no app install, no download, opening straight from a WhatsApp link. That makes distribution as simple as one woman sharing a link with another. Mythri is free for individual women.

Growth starts where trust already exists. The first wave runs through our Hyderabad field pilot, where a physiotherapy facility seeing 200 patients a day and an on-the-ground coordinator connect Mythri with the women who need her. From there, Mythri expands across Telugu and Urdu speaking communities in India, then, with Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Bengali, and more Indian languages, to all of India, South Asia, and the diaspora in the US and the Gulf.

The underlying engine, culturally localized voice AI with a zero-hallucination clinical guidance architecture, extends to new languages and new women's health domains. In a later phase, Mythri's reach opens partnership and licensing opportunities with hospital networks, clinics, and women's health organizations across these markets.
